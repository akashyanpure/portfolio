03 // ai, explained

          
How AI actually works, and why it runs on data.

          
An LLM is a next-token predictor trained on data and grounded by data. Try it out below.

        


        

          
          

            

              demo 1
              
Next-token prediction

            

            
Text is split into tokens, each token attends to the others (self-attention), and the model outputs logits that softmax turns into probabilities. Temperature controls how adventurous sampling is.


            


            

              
              

            

            
hover a token to see its attention ↑


            


            

              
              
            

          


          
          

            

              demo 2
              
Retrieval-augmented generation

            

            
LLMs hallucinate when they lack context. RAG chunks your documents, turns them into embeddings, stores them in a vector index, retrieves the nearest neighbours to a question and grounds the answer with citations. Most of that pipeline is data engineering.

            01
            
Garbage in, hallucination out

            
Model quality is capped by data quality. Deduplication, freshness, lineage and access control matter more for production AI than prompt tricks.

          

          

            02
            
Embeddings are just data

            
Vectors need the same rigour as any table: versioned models, incremental re-indexing, backfills and monitoring for drift.

          

          

            03
            
Evaluate, don't vibe-check

            
Golden datasets, retrieval precision and recall, groundedness checks and guardrails turn a demo into a product people can trust.
